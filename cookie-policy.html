Cookie Policy

This page explains the browser storage and optional measurement technologies used on azryon.com, and how you can control them.

Current website behaviour: Google Tag Manager is blocked until you select “Accept optional”. If you choose “Essential only”, the Tag Manager container is not loaded. Your selection is remembered in browser local storage for up to 180 days.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored by a website in your browser. Websites may also use browser local storage, pixels, tags, scripts and similar technologies. This policy covers those technologies when used on the main AZRYON website.

2. Essential preference storage

The consent interface uses a browser local-storage entry named azryon-cookie-consent-v1. It records whether you accepted optional tags or selected essential-only operation, together with the time of your choice. The preference is treated as valid for up to 180 days. This entry is used only to remember your choice and does not itself load analytics or advertising.

3. Optional tags and Google Tag Manager

The website source includes Google Tag Manager container GTM-MMNMFP83. The container is loaded only after you select “Accept optional”. When you select “Essential only”, the container is blocked. If loaded, tags configured inside the container may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, measurement, advertising or other functions selected by the website operator.

The contents of the Tag Manager container are configured separately from the website files. The website source alone cannot confirm which individual tags are currently published, their cookie names, providers, purposes or expiry periods. AZRYON should keep this policy and any cookie inventory aligned with the live Tag Manager configuration. Until a tag is confirmed, this policy does not claim a specific cookie name or lifetime for it.

4. Your choices

On your first visit, you can choose “Accept optional” or “Essential only”. You can reopen the panel at any time using “Cookie Settings” in the website footer. If you withdraw a previous acceptance, the website updates the consent state and reloads so that optional tags remain blocked on subsequent page loads.

You can also clear local storage or block cookies through your browser settings. If you clear the preference, the consent panel may appear again on your next visit.

5. Third-party services and links

Some pages load web-font files from the jsDelivr content-delivery network. A request for those files may disclose technical connection information to the delivery provider, but the font request is not itself configured by this website as an analytics cookie. Links to social networks, WhatsApp, the AZRYON Store and other websites connect you to third-party services. Those services may use their own cookies and privacy technologies once you visit them.

6. How long choices are kept

The consent preference is stored in your browser for up to 180 days. Optional cookies or identifiers created by tags after you accept them may have different expiry periods set by the relevant provider. Those periods depend on which tags are active in the Tag Manager container and should be verified against its current configuration.

7. Updates to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when the website’s tags, technologies or legal requirements change. The effective date below identifies this version. We recommend reviewing this page whenever you revisit the website’s privacy choices.

8. Contact

For questions about cookies or tracking on this website, contact contact@azryon.com.
